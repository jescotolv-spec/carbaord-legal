Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how CarBoard ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and stores information when you use the CarBoard mobile application.

Information We Collect

We may collect and store the following types of information:

  • User Account Information (such as email address, name, phone number, role, and assigned locations)
  • Business Information (such as company name, address, phone number, and logo)
  • Customer Information (such as names, phone numbers, and company details)
  • Vehicle Information (such as year, make, model, VIN, and service-related data)
  • Work and Job Data (including notes, timestamps, assignments, and status changes)
  • Scan and Document Data (such as uploaded PDFs and extracted vehicle information)

How We Use Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and operate the CarBoard application
  • Manage customers, vehicles, and service workflows
  • Track work sessions and job progress
  • Store and retrieve scan and document data
  • Improve app functionality and reliability

Data Storage and Third-Party Services

CarBoard uses third-party services to store and process data, including:

  • Firebase Authentication (for user accounts)
  • Firebase Firestore (database storage)
  • Firebase Storage (file and document storage)

Data may also be stored locally on your device to support offline functionality.

Offline Use and Synchronization

CarBoard may allow you to create or modify data while offline. In these cases, information may be stored locally on your device and synchronized when an internet connection becomes available.

We do not guarantee that all data is immediately synchronized at all times. Recent changes may not be fully synced if the device is offline or experiencing connectivity issues.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Your data is only used within the application and its supporting services as described in this policy.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data. However, no method of storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
